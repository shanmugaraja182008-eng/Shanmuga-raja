About

I like ideas that help people with ordinary problems.

I'm Shanmugaraja, a first-year B.Tech student in Information Technology. I'm at the stage where every project teaches me something I didn't know I was missing.

My best lessons so far came from hackathons. With a team of three and a deadline the next day, you learn fast what matters: a clear idea, a website that works, and a way to explain it in a few minutes.

The themes I keep coming back to are social good, food, health and waste. I want my code to be useful to someone who will never read it.
